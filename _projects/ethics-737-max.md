## Overview
This project presents a comprehensive ethical analysis of the Boeing 737 MAX crisis, examining the technical, organizational, and regulatory decisions that led to two fatal crashes and the loss of 346 lives.

## Ethical Issues
Key issues analyzed include the use of single-sensor dependency in MCAS, lack of pilot disclosure and training, regulatory delegation of certification authority, and post-accident decisions not to ground the aircraft.

## Analysis Framework
The case was evaluated using the systematic engineering ethics framework introduced in MAE 4300, incorporating ASME ethical canons, stakeholder analysis, definitional clarification, and hierarchy of obligations.

## Reflection
This analysis highlighted the responsibility of engineers to prioritize public safety above cost, schedule, and organizational pressure, and reinforced the importance of transparency, independent oversight, and ethical courage in safety-critical systems.
