## Overview
This project focused on the design, analysis, and experimental evaluation of a wind turbine blade under constrained laboratory conditions. The objective was to maximize aerodynamic power extraction while satisfying geometric, structural, and manufacturability requirements.

## Design & Analysis
The blade was designed using Blade Element Momentum Theory (BEMT) with NACA 4412 airfoil data interpolated across multiple Reynolds numbers. An energy-weighted design wind speed of 5.04 m/s was selected using a Weibull distribution. Iterative refinement, root and tip smoothing, and structural stress verification ensured performance and printability.

## Testing & Results
The blade was fabricated via 3D printing and tested in a wind tunnel using a torque-brake system. Experimental results demonstrated a peak power output of 1.29 W, exceeding design predictions and validating the robustness of the modeling approach.
